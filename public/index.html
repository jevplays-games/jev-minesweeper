Two boards. One race.

Choose your first square. That coordinate opens on both boards, with no mines in its surrounding neighborhood. Layouts are independent, so an opponent reveal does not reveal your mines. Clear every safe cell first to win. Flags are optional annotations, not points.

Controls

Click or press Enter/Space to reveal. Right-click or press F to set or remove a flag. Use the Reveal/Flag switch on touchscreens. Press C or double-click a revealed number to chord: its adjacent flag count must match that number. Incorrect flags can make a chord explode. Use arrow keys to move keyboard focus.

Race outcomes

A mine ends that board, not automatically the race. The other player must still clear their board. Two clears in the same 100-millisecond finish interval are a draw. Two exploded boards, or neither board clearing by 15 minutes, are a draw. Resignation or 30 seconds disconnected from a started server match is a loss. Ready matches expire after two minutes.

JEV and local play

JEV receives visible board facts and bounded candidate actions, never hidden mines. The solver handles proofs and exact arithmetic; JEV supplies structured candidate judgments. Difficulty changes reasoning depth, not hidden information. Without a TypeSafe key, the opponent is explicitly a local heuristic. Offline practice is entirely browser-local and never ranked.

Trust and rankings

Server games keep seeds, mine layouts, official time, and outcomes on the server. Discord identifies the player; a verified slash-command launch establishes server and channel context. Ranked matches require Discord, configured JEV, verified replay, and no fallback or material scheduling miss. Clear-win rate is wins divided by all eligible matches, including draws. A loss or draw breaks the win streak.

Analytics and privacy

The report records accepted commands, progress, timing, deduction coverage, flag precision and recall, action yield, board structure, 3BV, lead changes, heatmaps, provider reliability, token usage, and calibration where forecasts exist. It does not record keystrokes outside the game, device fingerprints, or mouse trails. Raw operational logs omit credentials and IP addresses. Post-game truth-derived metrics never appear in an active match.

A legal replay proves rule consistency, not unaided human play. Board randomness is not a guarantee of equal puzzle difficulty. “Uncertain” means unresolved by the bounded solver, not proof that guessing was logically unavoidable.
